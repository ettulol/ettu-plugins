# Photos and invitations

Use live schemas for exact arguments. The same verified-owner services power the website and MCP. Use returned image/download URLs directly; approved public photos are delivered from the edge. An optional thumbnail_url provides a verified WebP bounded to 640 pixels per side for galleries; use image_url for full-size views and download_url for downloads. It is null until delivery completes and for private previews. Local resizing uses no AI or credits; deleting a photo also revokes and purges its thumbnail. Unpublished previews belong only to the creator: they are absent from public and guest feeds, and other users cannot open them through direct links or MCP. Each member has a saved `character_version` when recorded, which remains fixed if the character later changes. Older photos may not have recorded a version; do not infer one from the current profile. For a new photo, interview for missing choices one question at a time, reusing what the user already supplied. The Take photo chat action begins this interview; it does not create the photo or invite anyone yet.

Each new selfie/retake costs 35 credits. Groups/retakes cost the organizer 40 for two characters, plus 5 for each additional character; guests pay nothing. The first generation call returns a 15-minute `credit_quote_required` quote. Show its exact price and parameters, then obtain the user's purchase confirmation before retrying the same command, original arguments and request key with `credit_authorization={quote_id,confirm:true}`. The organizer's reservation precedes invitations and work. A decline, expiry or cancellation before submission releases it. A delivered private preview remains charged even when disliked, replaced or never published. A selfie and two delivered retakes cost 105. Publication is free. One image submission is allowed per photo; never redraw automatically after quality, extraction, refusal, timeout or ambiguous failure. Resume saved output for delivery, or seek a fresh quote and confirmation for an intentional retake. `cancel_credit_operation` can release unused holds; submitted attempts settle on delivery or release within 24 hours. Top-ups do not restart expired work.


- Find an owned published character using `get_photo_booth_options(kind: "mine")`. No query shows the main character and three recent others. Search for additional characters.
- To add your other characters, use `get_photo_booth_options(kind: "mine", character: selected_primary_id)`. It excludes the primary and returns paginated active published characters in that universe. To find friends, use `kind: "friends"`, the primary ID and a nonempty search. Results identify `mine`. Never cross universes.
- Collect the organizer’s character, background, pose, guest roster and finally optional occasion before `create_photo_booth_photo`. Selfie has no extras; group has 1–5 additional characters. Put every additional ID in `invited_characters`. For each additional character you own, collect an explicit pose in `owned_character_poses`, keyed by its ID. Your characters join immediately without invitations or codes; never supply another creator’s pose. An all-owned cast queues one photo. Mixed casts invite only other owners, with the usual code checks and acceptance. All characters count toward the six-character limit and the group price. Sending invitations requires the user’s instruction. An intentional photo uses a new UUID `request_key`; uncertain delivery preserves the exact arguments and key.
- Preserve poses and hand assignments before choosing camera framing. If the organizer's action leaves no free hand (including separate props or bulky gloves), use an unseen photographer; otherwise use a handheld selfie. Infer hand use from the scene. Solo/group mode defines the cast, not who holds the camera. A hot dog in one hand and a foam fan glove on the other require the unseen-photographer framing. Keep that photographer and camera outside the frame. Never drop or merge props, simplify an action or invent limbs to free a camera hand. Middle-finger gestures, including giving the finger and flipping someone off, are not allowed; ask for a different pose.
- The rendering uses each participant’s published personality, favorites, dislikes, traits frozen for the photo. Scene-relevant differences can appear as expressions and small gestures while preserving every chosen pose and the background. Do not alter profiles, force a conflict or describe private prompts. Let the finished photo be the surprise.
- `get_activity_feed` includes invitations with the selected character and participant state alongside progress and photo results. `list_photo_booth_photos(invitations_only: true)` also reads pending invitations. `get_photo_booth_photo` reads one request and its participant state.
- `respond_photo_booth_invitation` accepts with `accept: true` and the user’s `pose` in the same call. Ask “What pose would you like your character to be doing?” if missing. Declining uses `accept: false` with no pose and cancels the whole group. Everyone must accept. The last acceptance automatically queues one image.
- The organizer may cancel only while invitations are pending. Accepted poses are final. Reactions never accept or decline invitations.
- Both selfies and group photos finish as private `awaiting_approval` previews. Read the exact preview as its organizer, then call `publish_photo_booth_photo(id, confirm: true)` only after the user says “I like it” or explicitly approves publication. Never transfer approval to a retake. Public `ready` photos cannot be retaken. Approval and its retries never generate. The creator-only image link expires; do not share it as public media.
- Approved photos are public. `list_photo_booth_photos` supports public, mine, favorites, album and in_progress scopes, universe filters and pagination. Mine automatically includes character appearances; favorites and album organization are private. To list every photo featuring one character, pass `scope: "public"` and `character: <character UUID>`; this includes selfies and group photos organized by anyone. Use `limit: 5` for the latest five, then follow `next_offset` with the same character/universe filters for the full newest-first list. The website character profile uses this read and links to a gallery with 24 photos per page. Private previews never enter that public list.
- Use `set_photo_reaction` for happy/love/shocked/sad/scared/laugh, `set_photo_favorite` for favorites, and the album tools for private organization. Album deletion removes only organization.
- Share the returned image/download/page links. Do not claim an external post occurred. Share with Friends uses device image sharing when supported, with a download fallback and Copy photo link always available. Share photo_url for a page link: approved public photos provide their own PNG and title for messaging-app previews. Private, unfinished and deleted photos expose no public preview image. The receiving app controls preview display and refresh timing. Reads, delivery retries, reactions, favorites and albums never start a new image generation.

If an invitee retries their exact accepted decision while the preview is creator-only, the response contains only `{id, recorded: true, decision, character_id}`. It confirms their own choice without exposing photo data.

Written poses stay private to their authors; background and occasion stay with the organizer. Never use these fields to deliver messages. Use `mark_activity_read` with the exact id/kind/status to acknowledge a requested notification, and `set_activity_reaction` with its message_id for a requested reaction. Direct messages, personal notes and replies are not supported. A user can create five custom albums plus the built-in Favorites collection.


For a requested organizer retake, use `retake_photo_booth_photo` with the source `id` and a fresh durable `request_key`. The source must be an unpublished `awaiting_approval` preview or failed photo and every participant must have accepted with a pose. Reuse all accepted poses privately; do not send new invitations or expose guest poses. Current published references are captured only while the same creators still own active characters in the same universe. The original remains private and superseded (`retaken_as` identifies the replacement); it cannot be published or retaken again. The new preview needs its own approval. After uncertain delivery retry the exact source id/key, even if either photo was removed.

`delete_photo_booth_photo` permanently removes the organizer's requested photo from everyone's galleries/albums and revokes the stored image. Receipts survive deletion to prevent resurrection. New photos have a generated title of at most six letters and a subtle embedded Ettu watermark that remains in downloads and shares.

Activity reads are bounded to 50 items: `get_activity_feed` accepts `offset`, `sort=desc` (newest first) or `asc`, and `kind=all` or a specific activity type. The total reflects the filter; notification counts remain global to the account.
